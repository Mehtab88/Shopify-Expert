Section 01

Executive summary

Big City Furniture has a real commercial foundation: a Columbia, SC showroom on Two Notch Road, a large wholesale catalog (ACME, Foagroup, Sealy, Tempur-Pedic), Synchrony financing, and Shop Pay. That combination can win store visits, financed tickets, and shipped orders.

The site is not converting that foundation. It currently reads like a manufacturer catalog uploaded to Shopify, not like a trusted local furniture retailer. Shoppers cannot quickly tell who you are, where you are, why they should buy here, what delivery costs, whether they can sit on the sofa in-store, or what happens if the piece does not fit.

Win Columbia–Midlands first. Then scale ecommerce on a cleaned subset of best sellers — not the raw 6,600-SKU dump.

Showroom + local delivery + financing + Sealy/Tempur, then photography, dimensions, and delivery promises on ~150 hero products.

Biggest leaks, in order of revenue impact

01 · Trust High

Homepage testimonials look generic. The Contact page still shows Shopify placeholder copy. Product pages have leftover theme text. There is no review app, no Google rating on-site, and almost no local proof.

02 · Invisible store High

The homepage never leads with Columbia / Two Notch / “visit the showroom.” There is no map, no store photos, no “see it in person” CTA, and no LocalBusiness schema.

03 · Product pages High

Titles are SKUs (Safi Sofa LV00216). Descriptions are wholesale bullets (Foam Density: D28). Dimensions, delivery, financing, and related pieces are missing. Shipping is “calculated at checkout.”

04 · Catalog IA High

/collections/all has 0 products. Duplicate and “Copy” collections are live. The Sofas collection includes sofa tables. 122 of 123 collections have empty SEO descriptions.

05 · Policies High

Returns must start within 2 days of delivery, with a 15–20% restocking fee. Mattresses are non-returnable. Hours conflict. Financing copy tells approved customers to visit the store.

06 · Paid media Medium

Shopping ads on 6,600 SKU-titled ACME products will get poor quality scores and likely Merchant Center issues. Meta catalog ads will look the same until the feed is cleaned.

Section 03

CRO audit

First impression, customer journey, product pages, and checkout. This is where furniture tickets are won or lost.

3.1 First impression & homepage

Verdict: weak for a high-ticket furniture purchase. A first-time visitor sees a modern Shopify storefront, a financing strip, and category tiles. They do not see a Columbia showroom, years in business, a Google rating, real customer photos, staff, or a reason to choose Big City over Ashley, Rooms To Go, Havertys, or Wayfair.

Trust damage on the live site

  • Contact page still contains Shopify’s default line: “Share information about your brand with your customers…” This signals an unfinished store.
  • About Us grammar error: “Thank you for allowing you to serve you!” Phone/email blocks also repeat many times and look broken.
  • Footer English is awkward: “Fast and reliable shipping on all US order.” / “PAYMENT SECURE.”
  • Homepage reviews are first-name + last-initial only (Sarah M., James T, Amina K., David R., Priya N., Michael B.) with no product, date, photo, or Google link. If they are not tied to real orders, they should come down immediately.

Above the fold

Current fold: announcement (“Shop Now Pay over Time” + hours) and a hero slideshow (“Trendy & Modern” / “Upgrade Your Home Today” / “Elevate Your Living Space”). The value proposition is interchangeable with any furniture URL. There is no city, showroom, offer, phone, or commercial incentive.

Value proposition

The site never answers the three questions furniture shoppers ask in the first five seconds:

  1. Is this a real store I can visit? Yes — but you hide it.
  2. Do you deliver / assemble / finance? Partly — scattered across FAQ and a second shipping page.
  3. Why not Ashley or Wayfair? 30+ years, local showroom, brand mattresses, Synchrony, white glove — none of this is packaged.

“Modern • Stylish • Built to Last” and “Top-tier quality” are adjectives, not a proposition. Test: Midlands furniture showroom with nationwide shipping, proved by Sealy/Tempur, ACME/Foagroup breadth, Synchrony, and local white glove. Offer free local delivery over $X or as low as $XX/month.

3.3 Product page CRO

Representative PDPs reviewed: Safi Sofa LV00216 ($2,350, ACME) and Paterson II Tight Top Ultra Firm (Sealy).

Images

Typical ACME items have 4–10 images — enough quantity, not enough selling. Missing versus converting furniture sites: lifestyle photography, scale reference, fabric close-ups, dimension overlays, video, floor-sample photos, and an “On display now” badge. Mattress pages are closer to usable because Sealy provides marketing copy. Upholstery pages look like a dealer portal.

Medium 90-day photo plan: shoot 20 showroom vignettes and attach them to matching SKUs. Even 50 SKUs with real photos will outperform 6,000 white-background SKUs in ads and on-site conversion.

Descriptions & pricing

High Safi Sofa description, in full: “Foam Density: D28.” That is also the meta description Google will show.

Other ACME examples are wholesale spec dumps (“Kd Legs”, “Table Top Thickness: 18mm”). These do not sell and fail SEO. Template for every hero SKU: 2-sentence shopper intro; spec table (W × D × H, seat height/depth, weight, materials, color, assembly); “fits a ___ room”; care/warranty; delivery method; financing line; “See this collection at 3010 Two Notch Road.” Do not put the SKU in the H1. Use Safi Leather Sofa and keep LV00216 as SKU/MPN.

  • Many prices end in .50 ($3,112.50, $822.50). Round hero SKUs to $X9 or $X99.
  • Set cards show $150.00 – $1,617.00 (e.g. FARSUND 4-pc). Show “Starting at” with the complete-set price emphasized.
  • No monthly-payment display next to a $2,350 sofa. That is mandatory.
  • Only show compare-at when true — do not fake MAP violations.

Financing, reviews, delivery, add-to-cart

The Financing page says approved customers receive a temporary account number they can use to shop in-store. Online shoppers who pre-qualify are told to drive to Columbia. If Synchrony can be used online, put it on the PDP. If not, use Shop Pay Installments / PayPal Pay in 4 online and Synchrony as the showroom closer — and say so.

No Judge.me / Stamped / Loox / Yotpo / Okendo detected. Product schema has no aggregateRating. Furniture conversion without reviews is possible in a showroom and very hard online at $1,000–$4,000. Install a review app and auto-request 10–14 days after delivery. Never buy fake reviews.

PDP line today: “Shipping calculated at checkout.” For furniture, that sentence is a bounce. You already have a fuller story on /pages/shipping-policy (white glove vs LTL vs parcel). A second policy at /policies/shipping-policy does not mention white glove. Two policies, two timelines (FAQ 3–7 days vs policy 7–14). Put a delivery module on every PDP: local white glove, statewide SC, national freight, measure-your-space, adult must be present.

Hide quantity and “Default Title” when there is one option. Fix mattress label “SIze.” Add inventory honesty (ships 3–7 days vs special order 4–8 weeks). Related products empty means AOV add-ons never appear.

3.4 Checkout & conversion

Shopify checkout (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay) is usually fine. The leaks are before checkout and in the policies next to it.

  • Shipping cost and method appear too late
  • No pickup-in-store option (huge for a Two Notch showroom)
  • Financing is not a checkout payment method based on Financing page copy
  • Cart is generic — weak trust stack
  • High-ticket carts feel like fashion checkout, not furniture (no access questions / appointment)

Likely abandonment reasons on this site

  1. Freight shock at checkout
  2. “Can I return this?” → 2 days + 15–20% restocking
  3. Leather / size unanswered
  4. Unsure if the store is real
  5. Want to finance and cannot finish online
  6. Delivery date unknown
  7. Mattress non-returnable with no trial
  8. Phone hours conflict; Sunday closed and not obvious
  9. Need spouse approval — no email/SMS reminder (no Klaviyo detected)
  10. Ashley/RTG show monthly price and free delivery thresholds

Industry furniture abandonment is already 70–85%. This site adds extra, avoidable reasons.

High Terms still say customers visit BigCityFurniture.com — wrong domain. Live domain is bigcityfurniturestore.com. Return window is far outside US furniture norms (typically 14–30 days for stocked items). Align damage policy (FAQ 48 hours vs refund policy). Explain Glory Movement LLC in one line on About so charge descriptors do not scare people.

Do now: cart ZIP estimate, local pickup, payment icons, confirmation with delivery window and damage instructions, Klaviyo abandoned cart/checkout. Owner decisions: 14-day return request on stocked furniture; mattress trial honesty before add-to-cart; one shipping policy; one hours line including Sunday Closed.
