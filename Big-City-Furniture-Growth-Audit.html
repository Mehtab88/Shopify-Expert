Executive summary

The site looks like a dealer catalog, not a Columbia retailer. Shoppers cannot tell you have a showroom, what delivery costs, whether a sofa will fit, or why they should choose you over Ashley, Rooms To Go, or Havertys.

Four issues are costing you visits and sales right now:

  1. The store is invisible. No map, no “see it in person,” no LocalBusiness markup, and a third-party listing at 9005 Two Notch Rd #0184 that does not match 3010.
  2. Product pages cannot close a furniture sale. Titles are SKUs (Safi Sofa LV00216). Specs read like a wholesale sheet (“Foam Density: D28”). Shipping is “calculated at checkout.”
  3. Trust is unfinished. The contact page still has Shopify placeholder copy. Product pages say “Describe your recently viewed products here.” Returns are 2 days with a 15–20% restocking fee.
  4. The catalog is messy. /collections/all has 0 products. Sofas include sofa tables. 122 of 123 collections have no SEO text. Duplicate “Copy” collections are live.

Recommended focus: win the Midlands first — showroom, local delivery, financing, Sealy/Tempur — then advertise a cleaned set of ~150 best sellers. Do not put the full catalog into Google Shopping as it stands.

CRO findings

Homepage

Above the fold is “Trendy & Modern / Upgrade Your Home Today.” That could be any furniture URL. There is no city, no offer, and no reason to visit 3010 Two Notch. “Pay over Time” is the strongest line on the page and it is treated as a strip, not a number ($X/month or 0% for X months).

Fix now

  • Lead with Columbia showroom + delivery. Two buttons: Shop living rooms / Get directions.
  • Correct Dinning. Remove duplicate FAQs (brand question is pasted twice).
  • Replace first-name testimonials with Google rating or take them down until they can be verified.
  • Put a real offer in the bar: free local delivery over a threshold, or a Synchrony term you actually have.

Stop doing

  • Mixing sofa tables into “top living collections.”
  • Prices rendering as $3,112.50 / — looks broken.
  • Sending people to /collections/all (zero products). Use /collections/all-products.
  • Empty Luxury Bedroom and Bed Frames collections in the menu path.

Product pages

Reviewed Safi Sofa ($2,350) and Sealy Paterson II. Mattress copy from Sealy is usable. ACME upholstery is not. A $2,350 sofa with one bullet and no dimensions will not convert, locally or nationally.

Trust and conversion

  • Rewrite the contact page. Add address, map, hours, click-to-call. The Shopify default sentence has to go.

  • Enable pickup for floor samples. Many tickets will still close on the phone — treat 803-500-0101 as a conversion, not a footer extra.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Ashley (Harbison, newly renovated), Rooms To Go, Havertys, and Kimbrell’s already own the obvious searches. You can still win Two Notch, Northeast Columbia, bunk beds, Tempur-Pedic, and financing — but the site barely claims a location. Homepage title is “Big City Furniture.” Meta description is missing. Schema is Organization only, not FurnitureStore.

GBP — first 14 days

  • Verify 3010 Two Notch Road (confirm this is the public showroom first).
  • Category: Furniture store. Hours matching the site. Phone 803-500-0101.
  • 20 showroom photos, cover of the storefront, 8 Q&As, weekly posts.
  • Correct or suppress the 9005 Two Notch listing if it is not you.
  • QR at the counter. Target 8–12 Google reviews a month until you pass 50.

On-site local

  • NAP in the footer on every page.
  • Showroom page with map, parking, brands, directions from I-20 / Fort Jackson.
  • Short pages for sofas, bedroom, mattresses, bunks (you have 217), financing, Lexington / Irmo / West Columbia.
  • You have unusual bunk depth — use it. PCS / Fort Jackson is seasonal demand the chains are slow on.

Do not launch Shopping on 6,600 ACME titles. A feed that advertises “Chateau De Ville Sofa W/5 Pillows (Same LV01588) 58265” with “Foam Density: D28” will waste spend and may fail Merchant Center quality checks.

Implementation roadmap

Goal for this window: more showroom visits, more phone leads, and a storefront that can take an ecommerce order without surprises. Paid media stays local until the feed and tracking are clean.

Week 1 — Tracking, blockers, GBP

  • Confirm GA4, Google Ads tag, Meta pixel, and enhanced conversions. Add call and direction events. Test a purchase and a call.
  • Contact, About, placeholders, typos (Dinning, SIze), Terms domain, single hours line.
  • Unpublish empty/Copy collections. Fix /collections/all. Footer NAP + schema.
  • Verify Google Business Profile at 3010 Two Notch. Upload photos. Seed Q&A. Put a review QR at the register.

Week 2 — Homepage, PDPs, trust, navigation, SEO

  • New homepage fold: Columbia, two CTAs, real offer. Remove unverified quotes or replace with Google stars.
  • PDP template on the top 50–100 SKUs: human titles, dimensions, delivery, financing, related items.
  • Retag sofas vs tables. Collection descriptions on Sofas, Sectionals, Bedroom, Dining, Mattresses, Bunks.
  • Cart: ZIP estimate and pickup. Search filters if Search & Discovery is available.
  • Showroom landing page. Klaviyo flows live.

Weeks 3–4 — Ads, remarketing, local pages

  • Google: brand + local search (store, sofas, bedroom, mattress, bunks, financing). Call extensions. Negatives for jobs, used, DIY, rental.
  • Shopping only after 150 SKUs have clean titles and GTINs/MPNs. No full-catalog dump.
  • Remarketing lists: site visitors, PDP viewers, cart. Furniture cycle is 1–6 weeks — budget for that.
  • Meta: Columbia geo, showroom Reels, PDP retargeting. $30–50/day is enough to learn.
  • Local pages: mattresses, bunks, financing, Lexington/Irmo. GBP posts weekly. Keep asking for reviews.

Weeks 5–6 — Read the data, then scale

  • Pause search terms and SKUs that spend without calls or sales. Raise bids on queries that drive directions and phone leads.
  • Compare PDP add-to-cart on the cleaned 50 vs the rest of the catalog. Double down on what moved.
  • Tighten the journey: related products, announcement offer, delivery copy on every hero SKU.
  • Scale only winners — local keywords, showroom creative, and SKUs with photos and dimensions. Leave PMax and national Shopping for after this sprint.
